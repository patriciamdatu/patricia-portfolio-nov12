DESIGN PROCESS

My design process began with research and strategy, ensuring the brand resonated with modern outdoor enthusiasts who balance urban life with adventure. I started by defining the brand’s core values, focusing on exploration, sustainability, and versatility. From there, I sketched initial logo concepts, experimenting with typography and iconography that reflected both rugged landscapes and a sleek, modern aesthetic. After refining my designs, I moved into Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, creating a strong visual identity that translated well across different mediums. I then developed the brand book, outlining guidelines for typography, color schemes, imagery, and logo usage to maintain consistency. I also established do's and dont's to ensure proper brand representation across digital and print platforms.
